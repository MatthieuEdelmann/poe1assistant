# Cluster jewels

Apply the [shared pricing method](../pricing.md).

- Identify jewel size, small-passive enchant, number of added passives, item level and
  rarity. These define the base market before any rolled notable is considered.
- Match the poe.ninja `ClusterJewel` enchant, passive-count variant and item-level bracket.
  Treat the row as a base reference; it does not price every finished rare cluster.
- Resolve the current enchant options, passive-count filter and notable stats through
  trade metadata. Option values are identifiers, not text to invent from an old example.
- Price the unrolled base first, then desired notables and other useful modifiers together.
  Verify the required item level for valuable effect/speed rolls before comparing bases.
- Check whether the build benefits from fewer or more passives. Do not universally price
  a smaller passive count as better or infer value from the number of jewel sockets alone.

An expensive finished listing does not prove that its notables drive the price: the enchant,
passive count or crafting threshold may already account for most of the asking price.
Do not infer crafting probabilities from the distribution of market listings.
