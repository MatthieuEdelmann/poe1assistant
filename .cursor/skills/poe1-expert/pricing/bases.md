# Crafting bases

Apply the [shared pricing method](../pricing.md). Use [clusters](clusters.md) for cluster jewels.

- Match base type, item level, influence, rarity and special state. In poe.ninja `BaseType`
  rows, inspect `levelRequired`, `variant` and `detailsId` together to identify the tracked
  item-level bracket; do not interpret the bracket as a finished item's equipment requirement.
- Verify the item-level threshold of the desired modifier before combining brackets.
  Being one level below a valuable crafting threshold can change the market entirely.
- For fractured bases, filter both the fractured state and the specific fractured modifier
  with its roll. An ordinary explicit modifier is not equivalent to a fracture.
- For synthesised bases, match the actual implicit combination. For influenced bases,
  include each relevant influence and distinguish single from double influence.
- Compare open crafting possibilities, corruption/mirroring restrictions and usable sockets
  where relevant. Existing mediocre affixes do not necessarily erase a base's crafting value.
- Current drop restrictions and item-generation tiers can affect supply. Verify them from
  current sources rather than carrying forward a fixed list of rare bases from old notes.

If the available poe.ninja row has weak coverage or a different influence/state, move to
trade instead of borrowing its price.
