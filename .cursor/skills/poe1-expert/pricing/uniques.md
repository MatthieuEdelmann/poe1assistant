# Unique items

Apply the [shared pricing method](../pricing.md).

- Match the exact unique name, variant, links and corruption state in poe.ninja. Inspect
  all matching rows instead of taking the first price. A generic quote describes neither
  perfect rolls nor special corrupted implicits.
- Resolve the base through trade item metadata, or search by unique name alone. Do not guess
  a base from the artwork or familiar item family.
- Add valuable rolls, socket/link configuration and corrupted implicits as separate steps,
  then combine them. Check whether a legacy version is relevant to the selected league.
- For Foulborn items, verify the current mutation filter and the actual mutated modifier.
  A mutation flag alone does not identify the variant being valued.
- For Watcher's Eye and other variable-mod uniques, compare the useful combination of
  modifiers, not just the name or number of modifiers.
- For Timeless Jewels, retain the seed and conqueror identity. Confirm the relevant effects
  in a compatible PoB or other verified source; the generic poe.ninja row cannot price a
  particular seed's build value. Resolve current seed filters through trade metadata.

Price a six-link premium against the same variant and corruption state. If discussing
linking profitability, use current material costs and distinguish a deterministic bench
cost from a probabilistic crafting estimate.
